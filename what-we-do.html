What we do

We collect your unwanted fidget spinners for processing in our Dublin facility. We break them down into their main components: plastic, weights, and ball bearings.

Where Your Donations Go

The recycled components from your fidget spinners are sold to a wide variety of industries. They can be transformed into parts for:
Toys
Sliding drawers
Musical Instruments
Fishing gear
And almost anything you can imagine!
