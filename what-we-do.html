What we do

We collect your unwanted fidget spinners for processing in our Dublin facility. We break them down into their main components: plastic, weights, and ball bearings.
The recycled components from your fidget spinners are sold to a wide variety of industries. Transformed into parts for:
Toys Musical instruments Sliding drawers Fishing gear
